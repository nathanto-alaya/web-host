The story bank

Real Alaya material, pulled from the purchase register, the client story packs, the founder scripts and the 2027 modelling, then sequenced against the campaign. Every story carries its source, its verification status and the job it does in the funnel.

01What makes an Alaya story land

Drawn from the stories that already perform, not invented rules.

  • The belief comes before the number. Every strong story in the archive opens on something the reader already believes, then breaks it. "She was told she needed $600,000" beats "client buys for $337,000" every time.
  • Somebody was against it. The best-performing stories all contain a doubter: family, a broker, a podcast listener's own scepticism, the whole market. The doubter is what makes the result mean something.
  • Adi was scared too. The stories where he admits the business was 1 month old, or that he had no conviction 2 years ago, outperform the ones where he is certain. Certainty reads as sales, doubt reads as a person.
  • One number, placed late. The archive's own house rule is no number in the opening line. The figure lands after the reader is already in the story.
  • Name the thing that was given up. A rejection, a walk-away, a market Alaya said no to. The rejections build more trust than the wins, and Alaya has better rejection material than almost anyone.

The spine that ties every email to this webinar: the deadline being advertised everywhere already passed on 12 May 2026, so the question stopped being when and became what. Each email below is one angle on that same turn.

Every ad in their feed says buy before July 2027 to keep negative gearing. That door shut on budget night, 12 May 2026, at 7:30pm. Anyone buying today is already inside the new regime. There is nothing left to beat.

Send 6 · T minus 6

The new build carve-out is worth $73,931, and it still loses

Alaya modelling
The turn

New builds keep negative gearing under the new rules. That is worth $73,931 against wages, which sounds like the end of the argument until you run the break-even. It is a growth gap of only 0.3% a year. At a 0.5% gap the new build finishes $24,006 behind. At Alaya's working assumption of 1.5%, it finishes $109,435 behind, which is 30% of the whole result.

The job

This is the webinar's second worked example, previewed. It gives the session something specific to promise rather than a general framework, which is what the registration page needs.

Verified

The Brisbane rehearsal

Verified
Detail

In 2018 you could not give a Brisbane apartment away. Approvals had collapsed to 46% below the decade average while sentiment was still terrible. Over the following 5 years those apartments rose between 71% and 117%, at one point moving 16% in a single year, faster than houses.

The 4 signals were a flat decade, missing supply, rising demand and very low vacancy. Alaya's stated position is that Melbourne apartments show the same 4 now.

Answers

"That market has been terrible for years." Carry the caveat Alaya already uses: no 2 markets are identical and this is not a promise Melbourne repeats Brisbane.

05Before anything ships

  • Names out, always. Several source emails carry client first names. Suburb and state plus numbers tells the story just as well, and that is already the house rule.
  • Separate valuations from estimates. Bank and independent valuations can be stated as valuations. Everything else is an estimate from an automated field or comparable sales and must say so. Mixing the two is the fastest way to lose the credibility this campaign is built on.
  • No per-deal "below market" claims. The register holds no suburb median at settlement, so a percentage-under-market figure cannot be supported deal by deal. The agency-level averages are separate and are sourced from the results page.
  • 3 number conflicts to resolve first. A Prahran unit appears at both $306,000 and $315,000. A block of 3 units appears as both $760,000 with offers at $1.19m and $705,000 with a unit selling at $355,000. The Melton hospital appears as both $900 million and about $1 billion.
  • Do not present the 16-unit Glen Huntly block as an Alaya purchase. The original email states plainly that it was a sale Alaya tracked and commented on, not a deal it did.
  • General information only. Alaya holds no AFSL and is not a registered tax agent. The tax content explains the law and shows the arithmetic, and stops there.
