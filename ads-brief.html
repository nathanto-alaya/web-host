04Paid ticket or free: recommend free for October

A change of position worth stating openly, since we leaned paid in writing on 15 September.

The case for charging

  • Filters the tyre kickers. The June ads review found roughly 80% of leads were unqualified and eating sales time.
  • Show up rates climb sharply once someone has paid, even at $19.
  • A ticket that credits toward the strategy package is a clean bridge into the offer.

Why not this one

  • A checkout step between ad and registration will lift cost per registration, and we have no baseline at all to predict by how much.
  • Meta optimises on conversion volume. Fewer conversions means slower learning, and a hard 20 October date leaves about 1 week to fix a funnel that is not working.
  • We would be testing a new landing page, a new topic, cold traffic and a paywall all at once. If it underperforms we will not know which of the 4 caused it.
  • The strategy package price and inclusions are not finalised, so we would be charging for a webinar whose offer does not yet exist.

The proposal: free registration on 20 October so we get clean cost per registration and show up data, and move the money to the offer inside the webinar. Test a paid ticket on the November session or the evergreen eWebinar version, where there is no hard date and it can run until it works. Frame it as earning the right to charge by getting 1 clean data point first.
